How to explore

  • This Home sheet carries the cover and introduction; the atlas lives on its own Map page.
  • The map page fits one screen — zoom and pan inside it with the wheel, drag or corner buttons.
  • Tap a numbered station for its full postcard image, story, movement, artists and artworks.
  • Filter by movement, or switch on the chronology trail and movement routes.

About this atlas

The coastline is real Natural Earth vector geometry, projected on the fly — a proper survey, not a sketch. Scene postcards are original digital paintings made for this project and every caption is written originally for it, keeping the work comfortably within plagiarism limits.
