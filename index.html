How to explore

  • This Home page carries the cover details and introduction; the gallery lives on its own Timeline page.
  • On the timeline, drag the track sideways, swipe, use Shift + scroll, or press ← →. Normal page scrolling is never blocked.
  • Click any card to open the full exhibit: story, context, materials, museum.
  • Inside an exhibit, ← → move between works, Esc closes.

About the replicas

Every object shown here is a digitally rendered virtual replica created for study — not a photograph of the original. Each exhibit lists the real artefact, its find-spot and the museum that holds it, so you can compare replica and original side by side. All interpretive text was written originally for this project.
